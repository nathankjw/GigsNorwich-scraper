Live music and gigs in Norwich

Norwich Gigs is a guide to live music in Norwich, bringing upcoming gigs from venues across the city into one searchable list. Search by artist, date or venue, switch between the compact list and the detailed poster view, and add any gig straight to your calendar.

Venues we list include Adrian Flux Waterfront, UEA LCR, Voodoo Daddys Showroom, Norwich Arts Centre, Epic Studios, Gonzo's Tea Room, The Holloway, Yalm, Dead Wax, Maddermarket Theatre and The Halls, plus pubs and bars across the city.
